How it works

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no charge for the collection and no catch.

Do I need to be at home?

No. On your collection day, leave your bags on the doorstep or somewhere we can see them from the street.

What if the date does not suit me?

The email with your date has a button to choose another day. You can also reply to any of our emails.

How will I know when you are coming?

We send a reminder the evening before, and on the morning of your collection we email you a 2-hour arrival window.

Is there anything you cannot take?

Please do not leave out anything wet, mouldy or soiled. If you are unsure about an item, add a note to your booking.

Who are you?

SUBNEX is a local South Wales business collecting unwanted clothes, shoes and bags across Cardiff, the Valleys, Caerphilly and Newport.
